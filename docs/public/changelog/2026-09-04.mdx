---
title: "GLM 5.3 and GLM 5.3 Flash"
date: "2026-09-04"
---

Z.ai's GLM 5.3 and GLM 5.3 Flash are now built-in models, available through
Z.ai, OpenRouter, and Fireworks. GLM 5.3 becomes the Z.ai default model and
takes over the portable `glm` and `glm5` aliases; GLM 5.2 remains selectable
as `glm-5.2` (`glm52`, `glm5.2`). GLM 5.3 Flash is a vision-capable small
default at $0.15/M input and $0.50/M output, with effort controls `low`,
`high`, and `max` on both models.
